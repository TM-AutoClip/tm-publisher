# Bluesky

[Türkçe](bluesky.tr.md) · [← Back to the README](../../README.md#connect-your-accounts)

TM Publisher talks to Bluesky only with **your own** developer app or key. Nothing goes through a server of ours; there is none.

## What you can post

Text (300 characters), up to 4 photos or one video.

## Steps

1. In Bluesky open Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords → Add App Password. Never use your main password here. <https://bsky.app/settings/app-passwords>
2. Give it a name (for example "TM Publisher") and copy the generated password (xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx).
3. Write your handle and paste the app password.
4. Leave "Server" as https://bsky.social unless your account is on another server (PDS).
5. Click "Connect and test".

When the test succeeds the account appears under **Accounts** and can be used in posts and groups.

## Good to know

- Text up to 300 characters; up to 4 photos (2 MB each) or one video (50 MB). Links and hashtags are marked so they stay clickable.
