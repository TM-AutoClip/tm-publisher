# Telegram

[Türkçe](telegram.tr.md) · [← Back to the README](../../README.md#connect-your-accounts)

TM Publisher talks to Telegram only with **your own** developer app or key. Nothing goes through a server of ours; there is none.

## What you can post

Text, photos, videos and albums to a channel or group through your own bot.

## Steps

1. In Telegram open @BotFather, send /newbot and choose a name and a username; BotFather answers with a token. <https://t.me/BotFather>
2. Paste that token into "Bot token".
3. Add the bot to your channel or group as an administrator with the right to post messages.
4. Write the channel's @username (public channel) or its numeric id into "Channel or group".
5. Click "Connect and test": TM Publisher reads the bot and the channel.

When the test succeeds the account appears under **Accounts** and can be used in posts and groups.

## Good to know

- Files up to 50 MB (photos 10 MB), captions up to 1,024 characters; Telegram's limit for bots.
