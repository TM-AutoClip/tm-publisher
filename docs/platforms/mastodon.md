# Mastodon

[Türkçe](mastodon.tr.md) · [← Back to the README](../../README.md#connect-your-accounts)

TM Publisher talks to Mastodon only with **your own** developer app or key. Nothing goes through a server of ours; there is none.

## What you can post

Text (500 characters on most servers), up to 4 photos or one video, with a visibility choice.

## Steps

1. Write the address of your server (for example mastodon.social).
2. Click "Connect and test": TM Publisher registers its own app on your server for your account (no approval needed) and opens the sign-in page.
3. Sign in if needed and click "Authorize"; the page shows a code.
4. Copy the code and paste it into the box that appears in this window.

When the test succeeds the account appears under **Accounts** and can be used in posts and groups.

## Good to know

- Limits come from your server; the usual ones are 500 characters and 4 attachments (photos or one video).
